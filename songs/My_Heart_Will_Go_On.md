---
title: My Heart Will Go On
---

Every night in Kervo,
I praise you, I hail you.
That is why I know you'll reign on.
Far below our footings,
in caverns of hellfire,
you will always guide us, Satan.

Near, far, wherever friends are,
I believe that they want to be killed.
Once more, I chop off their limbs,
and their bits are in bags,
and to jail I'll go on and on.
